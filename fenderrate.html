Why this matters

Published averages are based on standardized profiles — a 40-year-old with a clean record, specific coverage limits, and a 2023 mid-size sedan. Your real rate depends on dozens of factors those averages don't capture. Community data fills the gaps.

What happens with your data

Submissions are reviewed and aggregated. Individual responses are never published. Data that's significantly outside normal ranges for a profile is flagged for manual review before being included in estimates.
